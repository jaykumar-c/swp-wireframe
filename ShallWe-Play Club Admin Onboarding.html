Step 5: Completing the Pricing Plan Details (Step 3 of 3)

Input asset count, expected occupancy, payment model, and slot rates
/home/ → STEP 3 OF 3

When selecting "Self-onboarding — Fill in my details", the portal opens the pricing model calculator. Here is how to complete each field for your boxing club:

1
Exact number of assets (Courts/Fields) Enter the total number of bookable spaces inside your gym (e.g. 6 to 10 for Rings + Heavy Bag rows + Conditioning Studio).
2
Expected occupancy Select from the dropdown: 80% – 90% (or Less than 80% / 100%).
3
Booking payment type Choose between Paid Booking (all sessions paid via app), Unpaid Booking (free access/membership only), or Hybrid Booking (mix of paid drop-ins & included membership slots).
4
Booking price per slot (€) & Expected daily bookings Enter your standard slot price in EUR (e.g. € 15.00 to € 25.00) and expected daily sessions per asset (e.g. 8).
5
Hybrid split combination (if Hybrid selected) Choose the ratio of paid vs. member-unpaid bookings (e.g. 90% Paid – 10% Unpaid).
6
Click "Submit and Request Proposal" Click the blue button to submit your pricing structure to the ShallWe-Play operations squad.
