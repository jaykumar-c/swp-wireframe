      Actor 2

      Actor 3
      Club & Coach

      Welcomes players, keeps courts and rental rackets ready on-site, and provides verified coaching feedback that only updates a player's level if they explicitly accept.

      1. Quick and Simple

      Players get a working passport in under 60 seconds with 5 simple questions. No long registration forms. Quick checks after games take under 15 seconds.

      2. You Control Your Data

      All data is private by default. Wellbeing answers are strictly confidential. A coach note or level upgrade never changes a passport without the player's explicit approval.

      3. Grows From Real Games

      Passports grow automatically from real games, verified club play, and coach feedback — rather than guesswork or subjective rankings.

      Product Governance

      Open Product & Legal Decisions (Section 15)

      Strategic decisions recorded from Layer 1 and client briefings for stakeholder alignment.

      1. Layer 1 Baseline Decisions

      • First launch country: Netherlands (EUR) confirmed for initial rollout.
      • MVP sports: Padel, Tennis, Squash, Badminton, Football only. (Winter sports excluded).
      • Minimum age for child profile: Dependent on country rule.
      • Child direct login in MVP: Whether children log in directly or solely via guardian session.
      • Guardianship verification method: Validation via SMS, ID document check, or statutory declaration.
      • Dual guardian control: Equal rights vs primary guardian priority.
      • Guardian dispute process: Support mediation flow for contested guardian removals.
      • Mandatory profile fields: Kept strictly to country and age band at sign-up.
      • Verified achievement providers: Accreditation criteria for clubs and coaches.
      • Provider access expiration: Default sunset (6 months) vs indefinite until revoked.
      • Health data in Layer 1: Gated strictly by separate consent. No clinical diagnoses.
      • Accessibility storage: Stored on passport vs asked per booking.
      • Identity providers: Confirmation of Apple and Google sign-in integrations.
      • Login method: Single-use email code confirmed for MVP; passkeys in future phases.

      2. Legal & Strategic Alignment Items

      • Netherlands age cut-off for digital consent: Legal counsel review required (16 years under Dutch AVG implementation law vs 18 years legal adulthood). Display uses placeholder.
      • Journey status taxonomy: 12 status names (Layer 1) vs 9 phase names (Strategy deck). Wireframes provide the 12 Layer 1 names with proposed Dutch translations.
      • North star metric: Player Active Years Supported (Strategy deck) vs Sustained Meaningful Participation (Opportunity Map).
      • Partner compatibility presentation: Multi-dimensional overlap without overall scores or percentages.
      • Dutch journey status terminology: Review and sign-off on Dutch translations: Ontdekken, Proefperiode, Introductie, Leren, Regelmatig spelen, Verbeteren, Competitie, Rustfase, Blessure of herstel, Terugkeer, Overgang, Levenslang actief.
      • Scope of 'Share when I book': Confirm whether this rule applies exclusively to accessibility needs or extends to level and match history.
