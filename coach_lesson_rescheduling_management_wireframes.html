ShallWe-Play Coach Lesson Management — System Ground Truth & Architecture

Derived directly from the meeting understanding with Head Coach Jeroen Froma (House of Padel / SCO Sportcentrum Oudenrijn) and domain analysis.

1. The 5 Critical Operational Blindspots Resolved

1. Non-Cascading RRULE Integrity

When Session 2 is rained out, Week 3 does NOT shift to Week 4. Standard club calendars would break if 80 students had all upcoming weeks shifted. SWP isolates child session overrides while keeping the master series recurrence intact.

2. 8+2 Buffer Weeks Model (*Inhaalweken*)

Standard Dutch club coaching runs an 8-week cycle with Weeks 9 & 10 pre-reserved for catch-up sessions. This prevents coaches from scrambling for open court slots when bad weather hits.

3. €0.00 Court Hire Rollover Settlement

Because court hire was already factored into the 8-week season package, moving a lesson to an *inhaalweek* must NOT generate an additional €40 court charge. SWP records a €0.00 rollover settlement.

4. Atomic 5-Minute Court Lease Lock

Prevents race conditions where a public player or another coach books the inhaalweek court while the coach is reviewing student availability.

2. Formal System Resolution of the 7 Meeting Open Questions

Q1: Can a coach reschedule one session without changing the rest of the recurring series? [YES — CHILD OVERRIDE]

Yes. In data modeling terms, the LessonSeries defines the master recurrence (RRULE: FREQ=WEEKLY; COUNT=8), and individual LessonSession entities represent each week. When Session 2 is postponed, only Session 2's foreign key date is updated to Week 9. Sessions 3 through 8 stay locked on their original planned dates.

Q2: How are the two reserved weeks defined and managed in an eight-week series? [PRE-ALLOCATED BUFFER WEEKS]

When a coach creates an 8-week series, the platform automatically holds matching slots on Weeks 9 and 10 (*Inhaalweek 1* and *Inhaalweek 2*). If 0 sessions are missed by Week 8, the reserved slots are automatically released back to the club's open public booking pool 7 days in advance.

Q3 & Q4: Court Availability & Dual-Tier Approval Governance [DUAL-TIER MODEL]

SWP displays real-time court availability AND reserves the court. For Tier A (Head Coaches / Club Pros), confirmation executes immediately without waiting for front desk approval. For Tier B (Freelance Coaches), the reschedule request submits to the Club Manager's 1-click approval queue.

Q5: Multi-Stakeholder Automated Notification Broadcast [PUSH + ICS + BAANBEZETTING]

All 3 parties are synchronized automatically: (1) Enrolled cohort students receive instant push notifications and auto-updated .ics calendar invitations; (2) The club front desk court sheet (*Baanbezetting*) updates live; (3) The coach's mobile agenda updates with Stripe escrow release deferred to the replacement date.

Q6 & Q7: Unavailable Court Fallback & Court-Side Mobile Philosophy [FALLBACK COURTS + 1-TAP MOBILE]

If Court 3 has a conflict, SWP instantly suggests adjacent courts (e.g. Court 2 Indoor) or shifts to Reserved Week 10. On mobile, the coach gets a 1-tap "Rainout to Week 9" action directly on the active session card without entering complex forms court-side.
