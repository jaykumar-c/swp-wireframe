Business Strategy

Why Are We Doing This? (In Simple Words)

The clear story behind ShallWe-Play's business direction.

✕

What Didn't Work (Attempt 1 - 2025)

We tried to build a court booking and club management tool for sports clubs. It failed because clubs already have software they won't replace (like Matchi, Playtomic, and Sportlink). Clubs don't want another administrative tool.

• Booking tools only record 60-minute time slots.
• When a player stops booking, the club has no idea why they quit.
✓

The New Direction (Our Winning Idea)

"The club owns the season. The coach owns the lesson. ShallWe-Play helps the player keep playing for life." Instead of competing with court booking tools, ShallWe-Play becomes the player's personal sports buddy.

• We bring ready-to-play players to clubs and coaches.
• We notice when someone is losing interest and help them get back on track.
