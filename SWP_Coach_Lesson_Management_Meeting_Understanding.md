# ShallWe-Play: Coach Lesson Management — Meeting Understanding

## 1. Purpose

This document captures the understanding of the coach-related process discussed in the meeting transcript. It focuses on how coaches manage training lessons, recurring schedules, and rescheduling when a session cannot take place as planned.

> **Note:** This document records the meeting understanding. Potential system improvements are identified as possibilities, not as confirmed or finalized requirements.

## 2. Coach Lesson Scheduling

- Coaches manage training sessions and lessons.
- Lessons may be scheduled as a series across multiple weeks.
- Coaches need a calendar view to review their scheduled lessons.
- The meeting confirmed that the calendar is available in the same Training Manager mobile application.
- Coaches may need to change the date of a lesson within a scheduled series.

## 3. Rescheduling a Lesson

### Example discussed

If a training session cannot take place because of rain, the coach may want to move it to the same day and time three weeks later.

The coach needs to be able to:
1. Identify the affected lesson in the calendar.
2. Change the lesson date.
3. Check whether the required court is available.
4. Coordinate the change with the club as needed.

The transcript indicates that the coach can change a date and move it further, but it does not establish the complete set of rescheduling rules or confirm whether changing one lesson leaves every other lesson in the series unchanged.

## 4. Recurring Series and Reserved Weeks

An example was discussed in which a series runs for eight weeks and has two reserved weeks that can provide flexibility if a lesson is missed.

The discussion indicates:
- An eight-week lesson series may include two reserved/alternative weeks.
- If a lesson is missed, one of those available weeks may be used.

**Clarification required:** The transcript does not fully define how reserved weeks are represented, when they can be used, or whether they are automatically assigned or selected by the coach.

## 5. Current Club Coordination Process

The current process described in the meeting is manual:

1. A lesson needs to be moved.
2. The coach contacts the club, for example by email or through the relevant contact person.
3. The coach checks whether a court is available on the proposed date and time.
4. The coach can proceed with rescheduling after checking availability.

This means that rescheduling depends on communication between the coach and the club.

## 6. Potential ShallWe-Play Improvement

The meeting explored how SWP might reduce manual coordination. These points were discussed as potential improvements and should not be treated as confirmed scope without further agreement.

- If a coach is linked to a club, SWP could show or check the club's court availability.
- This could reduce the need for the coach to email the club just to check availability.
- SWP could send emails or notifications on behalf of the coach when a lesson is changed.
- Notifications could help relevant parties stay informed and provide confirmation of the change.

The transcript does not confirm the exact approval model, notification recipients, or whether an available court allows the coach to finalize a change without club approval.

## 7. Key Understanding

The core coach requirement discussed is:

**Enable coaches to manage recurring lessons and reschedule individual sessions while coordinating with the club's court availability and keeping relevant parties informed.**

The intended opportunity for SWP is to make this process more direct and reduce the manual emails or calls currently required between coaches and clubs.

## 8. Open Questions to Confirm

1. **Individual-session changes:** Can a coach reschedule one session without changing the rest of the recurring series?
2. **Reserved weeks:** How are the two reserved weeks defined and managed in an eight-week series?
3. **Court availability:** Should SWP display availability only, or also reserve/book the court?
4. **Approval:** If a court is available, can the coach confirm the reschedule directly, or must the club approve it?
5. **Notifications:** Who should be notified when a lesson changes (for example, the club, players, or other relevant parties)?
6. **Unavailable court:** What should happen if the requested court or time slot is unavailable?
7. **Mobile capabilities:** The meeting confirms a calendar in the Training Manager mobile application, but does not establish which rescheduling actions are supported on mobile.

## 9. Scope Boundary

This document captures only the understanding supported by the meeting discussion. It does not define final product requirements, acceptance criteria, technical design, or confirmed delivery scope. Those should be agreed after the open questions above are answered.
