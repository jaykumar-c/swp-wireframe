18 Business Rules Reference (Netherlands SnowWorld Standard)

1. The current date must be selected by default when entering the skiing booking flow.
2. The user must select an Indoor Snow Dome Asset before configuring pass.
3. Facility specs focus strictly on indoor snow dome metrics (Slope length, vertical drop, temperature -2°C, real snow, magic carpet counts, capacity).
4. Simplified pass selection exposes segmented duration (1h, 2h, 4h Evening, Full Day), 2-hour arrival windows, and quick gear bundle toggle.
5. Primary skier details configured for pass authorization.
6. Skier category (Adult, Youth, Child, Senior) selected per individual skier.
7. Lightweight optional lesson request module attaches unassigned data tag for front-desk coach allocation.
8. Adding additional skiers is OPTIONAL; user can book alone.
9. A. Book for friend (Primary Skier configures) OR B. Invite friend (Friend configures asynchronously).
10. Minimal equipment & locker selection without complex DIN binding or biometric forms.
11. Complete booking summary and itemized price breakdown displayed before payment.
12. Simple legal compliance via standard Terms & Conditions & Indoor Safety Rules checkbox.
13. Payment authorization securely tokenizes payment method (Card / Apple Pay / Google Pay / iDEAL mandate) for slope access & overtime settlement.
14. Confirmed booking generates high-contrast dynamic QR Code with Apple/Google Wallet integration.
15. Cold-hall turnstile scan logs slope_entry_timestamp and starts session countdown timer.
16. Exit turnstile scan logs slope_exit_timestamp, measuring total session duration without offline cashier visits.
17. 15-minute grace period applied. Overtime fee automatically charged to card-on-file; turnstile unlocks with green indicator.
18. Final completed state shows itemized receipt with base pass, rentals, lesson add-on, and auto-settled overtime charge, with instant invoice download.
