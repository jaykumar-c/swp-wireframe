Player Passport Platform

Mobile App & Club Ecosystem — Web & Mobile BRD

Prepared For: ShallWe-Play Team & Venue Partners

Current Business Problem

  • Long Onboarding Forms: Traditional sports apps in the Netherlands ask new players 10 to 15 questions before they can book a court or find a match. This takes 10+ minutes and causes over 60% of new players to leave before finishing signup.
  • Scattered Player Levels: A player's rating is often locked inside a single club ladder, official federation league (like KNLTB), or local WhatsApp group. When a player visits a new venue, they start as an unknown beginner, leading to mismatched and uncompetitive games.
  • Forced Public Information: Conventional sports portals make player phone numbers, skill ratings, and match history public by default. Privacy-conscious Dutch players hesitate to register when their personal information is visible without permission.
  • Unilateral Club Changes: Clubs and coaches sometimes change a player's level without asking them. When a club lowers or raises a player's rating without their consent, players feel frustrated and lose trust in the venue.

The Proposed Solution

  • Fast 60-Second Onboarding: New players answer at most 5 quick questions during signup to get their starting passport in under 60 seconds, so they can book or play immediately.
  • Passport Grows From Real Play: Instead of long forms upfront, the passport evolves naturally from real matches played, short reflections, and coaching notes.
  • Player Owns Their Level (The Golden Rule): A player's level changes only if the player accepts. Clubs and coaches can suggest adjustments, but the system never changes a rating without the player's explicit approval.
  • 100% Private by Default: Every new passport starts completely private. The player decides what is visible to others, and can hide or show individual sports anytime.
  • Quick 15-Second Match Reflection: After a session, players complete a short 4-tap check to rate enjoyment, level, and rematch preferences. Physical feelings are kept in a private log protected by clear GDPR consent.

Systems Impacted

  • SWP Player Mobile App (iOS & Android): Sign-up flow, 5 starting questions, player passport profile, court booking, post-match check, and club notes.
  • SWP Club & Coach Web Portal: Venue portal where clubs check in players, confirm court sessions, and submit coaching notes and level suggestions.
  • SWP Booking & Matchmaking Service: Core backend handling court bookings, player levels, location search radius, and rental add-ons (€3.50 racket hire).

Assumptions & Dependencies

  • The launch focuses on the Netherlands sports market.
  • The app defaults to Dutch with an easy English switch.
  • Padel is the primary sport for launch, with support for tennis and squash.
  • Adult players (18+) can register directly. Players under 18 must get parent or guardian approval via email.
  • All text on screens uses simple words with zero decorative emojis.

Business Requirements

Part A: Player Mobile App (ShallWe-Play App)

1. Account Setup & Safe Sign-In

  • Ability for players to sign up using email code, Google, or Apple sign-in.
  • Ability to receive a 6-digit code via email with a 60-second resend timer.
  • Ability to choose between an Adult account (18+) or a Child account (under 18).
  • Ability for child accounts to require parent or guardian email verification before access is granted.
  • Ability to set a display name with live checkmark validation and helpful suggestions.

2. 60-Second Onboarding (5 Starting Questions)

  • Ability for new players to answer at most 5 quick questions during signup.
  • Ability to pick what brings them to the app (play, improve, book, return, explore) with up to 2 selections.
  • Ability to select their main sport (Padel, Tennis, Squash).
  • Ability to choose their starting skill level (Beginner, Intermediate 3.5, Advanced, Pro).
  • Ability to select what matters most to them (fun, fitness, coaching, meeting people, safe courts) with up to 3 selections.
  • Ability to set their playing area by postcode and search radius slider (such as Amsterdam 1071 within 5 km).
  • Ability to skip questions and see a progress counter on every question screen.
  • Ability to see their trial passport immediately in under 60 seconds with a clear starting phase based on their answers.

3. Player Passport & Privacy Controls

  • Ability to view a clean personal passport card showing avatar, handle, sessions played, followers, and 4 strength tags without numeric scores.
  • Ability for the passport to remain 100% private by default until the player chooses to share it.
  • Ability for players to hide or show individual sports from their profile anytime.
  • Ability to edit personal details (name, photo, bio) anytime in one place.
  • Ability to view a timeline of past matches, lessons, milestones, and club notes with clear source labels.

4. Passport Growth & Weekly Question Prompts

  • Ability for the passport to grow automatically from real games, lessons, and verified club notes.
  • Ability to open an optional questions backlog anytime to complete extra profile details at the player's own pace.
  • Ability for the system to show at most one optional question card per week on the home screen.
  • Ability to see a clear "Why we ask" note and a Skip button on every optional question prompt.

5. Court Booking & Pre-Arrival

  • Ability to add racket rentals (€3.50) directly during court booking with transparent price breakdown.
  • Ability to mark if this is the player's first visit to a club.
  • Ability to answer an optional pre-arrival question about court adjustments before their first visit.

6. Post-Match 15-Second Reflection & Physical Wellbeing

  • Ability to complete a quick 4-tap reflection check in under 15 seconds after a match.
  • Ability to rate match enjoyment, level fit, physical feeling, and rematch preference with the group.
  • Ability for the system to ask for clear GDPR consent before saving any physical feeling data.
  • Ability to view past feeling check-ins in a private list and delete any individual record with one tap.
  • Ability for the post-match check to expire automatically after 24 hours so players are never bothered by old matches.

7. Club Notes & Player Level Rule

  • Ability to receive official coaching tips and notes from verified clubs on the passport timeline.
  • Ability for the system to enforce the Golden Rule: a player's level changes only if the player accepts.
  • Ability for players to choose between 4 clear actions when a club suggests a level change: Accept new level, Keep current level, Decline note, or Ask a question.
  • Ability to see verified badges on club notes so players know the feedback is authentic.
Part B: Club & Coach Web Portal

8. Player Check-In & Attendance

  • Ability for clubs to check in players for court bookings and group sessions.
  • Ability to mark session completion so matches count toward the player's passport activity.

9. Coach Feedback & Verified Level Suggestions

  • Ability for accredited coaches to submit feedback, tips, and session notes to a player's timeline.
  • Ability for coaches to suggest a level adjustment without being able to force-change it without player approval.
  • Ability to see whether a player accepted or declined a suggested level change in the venue dashboard.
